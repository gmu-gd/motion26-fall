Exercise 4

Sound and Motion

Description

Make a 15 second animation using the layered Illustrator file on Canvas and some kind of audio. Upload your final video to Vimeo, add it to the group on Vimeo, and submit the link to the video on Canvas for this assignment.

Download the Illustrator file

Requirements

  • 1920x1080
  • 15 seconds long
  • Uses audio
  • Nothing extra added to the composition

Things to Keep in Mind

  • The sound should reinforce the motion, not be a random extra
  • Be precise with timing

Objectives

  • Continue in After Effects
  • Begin to consider how sound and motion pair together

Final Submission

  • Turned in on time
  • All requirements met
  • Video file submitted on Canvas
  • Video uploaded to Vimeo and added to the class group
